## Moho pathing, flag on

These runs are evidence for roadmap P1's default-on decision; none is a gate.

**The gate's data tests with the flag on.** Every gate data mode was run as ctest runs it, with `--moho-pathing` added. 189 of 195 pass. Each of the six failures checks the grid pathfinder's behaviour, and each follows Moho's rules as faf-re gives them:

| Mode | Check | Why, with the flag |
|---|---|---|
| `drive-test` | T4, T7: stops 0.50 and 0.52 from the exact point | `CAiPathNavigator::UpdateCurrentPosition` goes Idle once the unit's cell is the path's last: it stops in the goal cell |
| `drive-test`, `weapon-test` | T5 and T9: an extra `Cruise>Stopped` before the move. `weapon-test` T11 counts T9's failure | while it searches, `CAiNavigatorLand::Execute` steers at its own cell's centre |
| `factory-rally-test` | roll-off 0.68 from the exact point | the goal-cell stop |
| `crowd-test` | T3: the idle tank isn't pushed | `func_IsSourceUnit` mode 1: the search routes round an idle unit the mover doesn't outrank |
| `steer-test` | T1, T3, T5: no sidestep; the units pass without contact by routing round | the search routes round them. Whether Moho's own steering would also sidestep here is open |
| `range-test` | T12: a launcher backing off stops at 4.57, inside its minimum range 5, and never fires (watched for 600 ticks) | `CUnitFireAtTask` backs off to 1.1 × MinRadius as a one-cell goal. Entered from the target's side, the cell's near edge is inside the range, and `IsAtPosition` compares cells, so the same goal comes back. Moho's own rules stall here too; retail would confirm it |

**Scenarios.** Large-unit gaps, an unreachable goal, a crowd and a formation turned up three port bugs at this head. #435 (open) fixes them:
- a move whose search failed searched again forever: a goal walled in all round, or a 3×3 unit at a gap two cells wide;
- the hold brake and the crowd separation could put a unit where its footprint doesn't fit;
- a sidestep placed inside a wall held the unit against it until its move ended.
