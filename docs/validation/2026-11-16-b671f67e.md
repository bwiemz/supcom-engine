## Goldens

**`scmp009_game_ui`, `scmp009_game_ui_minimap`, `build_drag` and `formation_drag` were re-recorded.** The old references, from 2026-10-13 and 2026-10-14, were kept in `golden/backup-2026-11-16/`.

They differed by 23.5%, 24.7%, 25.2% and 24.5% of their pixels, with a mean error of 9.4 to 10.6. Four causes account for every difference, each a merged PR:
- **The frame is darker** (mean brightness of the world area 63.0 → 59.1).
  - Cause: #750, landed in `6bb3b9c1` (#765, 2026-10-20). It draws the frame and the bloom into 8-bit targets, as FA does, where they had been 16-bit floats. Each pass now saturates at 1, and the bloom gets less glow.
  - Found by bisecting: each step built that commit, captured the golden scene, and judged it by the world area's mean brightness. 10-15's `6e61df5f` gave 62.97, the reference's level, and every commit from `6bb3b9c1` on gave 59.1.
  - The fog of war (#676) was ruled out: the frame with `--no-fog` is the same frame.
- **The clock** reads `00:00:09`, not `00:09`. #925 made `GetGameTime` give Moho's `HH:MM:SS`.
- **The warp-in's crater and smoke turned.** The random stream changed after the references were recorded (#1012's army starts, #1017, #1018).
- **The storage readout** is 4000 energy and 650 mass, not 4200 and 850. An army now holds no storage of its own, which matches the original game's start.

The drag goldens' contents are unchanged: the same wall line, and the same formation ghosts. The new references are pixel-identical (at most 1 level in a few pixels) to `b17c9c53`'s captures, so #1026 and #1027 don't touch these scenes.

## Benchmarks

`tools/bench.py run` on this build: four retail AIs on SCMP_009, seed 4242.
- Each game ran twice, in the order early, late, late-moho, late-moho, late, early. The render scenes ran once each.
- **The machine wasn't quiet.** Other projects' jobs ran throughout: a GPU job holding the GPU at 99–100% and 14.5 GB of its memory, and two processes taking a core each (load 3.0 to 4.4 on 16 threads).
- As a result, the two runs of a game differed by 8% to 11%, where the runner's clean runs of 2026-11-12 differed by under 1%. So these rows show where the time goes, not a baseline.

| Scenario | Game at the end | Measured (mean of two) | The two runs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `early`: 6,000 ticks, Moho pathing | `d8c5d22d`; 676 units, 42,230 entities; 7,064 searches, 3.2 M cells expanded | 28.0 s of sim; a tick's mean 4.66 ms, p50 3.41 ms, p99 21.2 ms; peak 443 MB | 26.5 s and 29.5 s |
| `late`: 18,000 ticks, the grid's pathing | `6fc510db`; 1,403 units, 47,736 entities | 264 s of sim; a tick's mean 14.7 ms, p50 14.4 ms, p99 57.7 ms; peak 572 MB | 251 s and 277 s |
| `late-moho`: the same, Moho pathing | `c2b91f74`; 1,668 units, 39,624 entities; 140,541 searches, 36.9 M cells expanded | 415 s of sim; a tick's mean 23.1 ms, p50 25.9 ms, p99 66.4 ms; peak 632 MB | 398 s and 432 s |
| `render-battle`: tick 6,000 to 6,120 | `ff9e472f` | CPU `render()` p50 4.32 ms, p95 6.80 ms; GPU p50 0.67 ms; 282 MB of VRAM | one run |
| `render-late`: tick 18,000 to 18,120 | `2bcc3f29` | CPU p50 3.81 ms, p95 7.29 ms; GPU p50 0.47 ms; 356 MB | one run |
| `render-strategic`: the same, zoomed out to icons | `2bcc3f29` | CPU p50 2.62 ms, p95 6.17 ms; GPU p50 0.34 ms; 356 MB | one run |

- **Moho pathing costs this late game 57% more sim time:** 415 s against 264 s.
  - The games differ. Moho's ends with 1,668 units against the grid's 1,403. On 2026-11-12 (runner, `9d632e3b`), Moho's ended with 1,365 against 1,494, and the cost was 7%.
  - Since #1012, every game is a different game. Moho's late game now holds more units and searches more (140,541 searches against 126,068), at about 260 cells a search.
  - A search's cost, and the navigator's and steering's per unit, are the place to look first.
- **The render frame on the RTX PRO 4500 takes about twice as long as on 2026-10-12** (main at `19c44e71`): CPU p50 4.3, 3.8 and 2.6 ms, against 1.7, 2.4 and 1.8 ms.
  - The busy GPU doesn't explain it: the CPU waited 0.01 ms a frame for it.
  - Most of the growth is `Render::unit_update`: 2.0 ms of the mean frame in the battle and late scenes, against 0.45 ms in the late scene's profile of 2026-10-06.
  - So the runner's 3–5× of 2026-11-12 is part code and part machine. On the same machine, the code since October has doubled the frame.
