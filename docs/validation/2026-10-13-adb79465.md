## Goldens

**`scmp009_game_ui`, `scmp009_game_ui_minimap`, `build_drag` and `formation_drag` re-baselined.** The old references were kept in `golden/backup-2026-10-13/`. They differed by 4.865%, 4.865%, 5.088% and 5.075%.

The differences were explained in #628 with diff maps and crops, which were reviewed before it was merged. This head's captures were pixel-identical to the reviewed ones. There are two causes:
- **The tree groups turned the right way.** A `.scmap` prop's axes are its rotation matrix's columns, and the engine had read them as rows. The groups' trees and their long shadows now stand where the original puts them. That accounts for the streaks in the top-right and bottom-right corners.
- **The random stream after the warp-in.**
  - The commander's warp-in knocks trees over with a Force ring, and the turned groups meet the ring differently. Within 40 units of the start it now breaks 7 groups into 160 trees by tick 89, where it broke 6 into 138.
  - A probe's random draw agrees between the two builds at tick 39 and differs at tick 89. So the warp-in's smoke, and the dust over the crater at 00:17, fall differently.
  - The commander and the crater decal are unchanged.

**Retail's AIs build as many units again.** At the 3be4d6a5 record seed 4242 had 260 units at tick 6,000. #615's `GetUnitsAroundPoint`, gathered as the original does (checked against it), brought it back: 639 here. Retail's script race (`aiattackutilities.lua:952`), seen in both default games then, didn't show in any game this time.

**FAF, seed 4242: `/lua/platoon.lua:1555: attempt to call method 'GetLocationCoords' (a nil value)`,** forked at `adaptive-ai.lua:373`. This is FAF's script at an AI's defeat.
- Army 1 was defeated at about tick 5,300. FAF's `SetDefeatStatus` calls `DisableAI` (`SimUtils.lua`), which sets every base manager's `EngineerManager` to nil.
- The ArmyPool platoon's `BaseManagersDistressAI` loops while its platoon exists. It isn't stopped, so its next pass calls `locData.EngineerManager:GetLocationCoords()` on nil. FAF gives nil a metatable, so the index reads nil and the call fails.
- Whether FAF's own engine reaches it too, for example by destroying a defeated army's platoons first, isn't established.

**FAF, seed 7777: `/lua/ai/transportutilities.lua:1025: attempt to perform arithmetic on field 'AirThreatLevel' (a nil value)`,** twice. This is an engine gap.
- FAF's transport code adds up the `Defense.AirThreatLevel` of the enemy units near a point, unguarded.
- FAF's blueprint loader computes threat levels only for units without `Defense.SkipDynamicThreatCalculations`. The skipped ones keep whatever their `.bp` says.
- Moho's `RUnitBlueprint` defaults all four threat levels to 0 (faf-re `RUnitBlueprint.cpp`), and the engine doesn't. A fix follows this record.

## Against the original game (2026-10-13, Seton's Clutch, Steam FA under Proton)

The questions only the original can answer were probed in both games with scratch hooks, the same way as `tools/moho_probes/`:
- **Tree breakup** (12 Strikers driven through a forest): 82% of the groups broken in the original, 87% in the engine. The rates match.
- **`GetUnitsInRect`'s order:** three units made in one cell come back `3 2 1`, and after one is warped away and back, `1 3 2`, in both. Integration Tests 10c to 10e pin it.
- **`GetReclaimablesInRect`'s membership:** 149 tree groups in one rectangle in both, with every collision extent within 0.001. Before #627 and #628 the engine found 127.
- **Map props' orientation:** all 30 groups compared had the conjugate quaternion before #628. Test 5b pins one group to the original's values.
- **Units' collision boxes:** turned units with a collision offset (four headings) are identical in both. A moving unit's box isn't widened to its skirt, so faf-re's reading of `Unit::UpdateCollision` isn't ported.

These probes aren't in `tools/moho_probes/` yet.
