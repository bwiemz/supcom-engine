## The commander at the start

The commander not being selected at the start was looked into before this record, and it is Moho's own behaviour at steady frame times:
- Moho's `SelectUnits` keeps only units whose `IsSelectable()` holds (faf-re `cfunc_SelectUnitsL`). That is a virtual, and `UserUnit`'s rejects `UNITSTATE_UnSelectable`, which the warp sets for 2.1 s from tick 30.
- Retail's gamemain selects once, 3 s of wall clock after the interface's first frame. Moho's user side applies beats on a clock that counts one frame more than that thread's, so tick 30 reaches it no later than the attempt. Only a hitch at the start, which holds back the beat clock (Moho feeds it the median frame time), lets the attempt land before the warp.
- FAF met it in 2015 (commit 9d77e1c083, "Make sure commander always is selected at start") and retries the selection every 0.1 s until tick 50. On FAF's data in the engine, its interface comes out of hiding at tick 40, and the last try (tick 51) is a tick before FAF's warp ends (52).

No engine change was made.

**Retail's race** is the one the [527e060c record](2026-10-08-527e060c.md) traced to retail's scripts: `/lua/ai/aiattackutilities.lua:952: Attempt to set attribute 'MovementLayer' on nil`, forked at `platoon.lua:2865`. It shows in both default games this time and in neither Moho one; it is a timing race in the AI's engineer platoons.

**The FAF error** is new: `/lua/platoon.lua:3720: attempt to call field 'SetupEngineerCallbacks' (a nil value)`, forked at `platoon.lua:3773`. `WatchForNotBuilding` calls `eng.PlatoonHandle.SetupEngineerCallbacks(eng)` when an engineer goes idle with builds still queued, and nothing defines that function: not FAF's Lua (the call is its only mention in FAF's repository) and not the engine (faf-re has no such name). It is dead code in FAF's scripts, reached once in this game.

**The retail AIs build about half the units they did.** At the bench's baselines (2026-10-07, main at 6d59aa3b) seed 4242 had 517 units at tick 6,000 and 1,418 at the end; here it has 260 and 874, and with Moho pathing 272 and 546 (581 and 1,570 then). FAF's AIs are unaffected (605–673 at tick 6,000). Before #591, at 6570e4c6, the same game has 540 at tick 6,000.

It comes from #591, and within it from one commit. #591's branch keeps its 83 commits, so `git bisect` ran over them, counting units at tick 6,000 (seed 4242, default pathing):
- the first bad commit is 0c2bead5, "aibrain: GetUnitsAroundPoint takes the units on its square's collision cells". The commit before it has 600 units at tick 6,000, and it has 286.
- Moho gathers `GetUnitsAroundPoint`'s units from its 4-unit collision cells under the square about the point, with no distance test (faf-re `func_GetUnitsAroundPoint`: `func_Rect2fToInt16`, then `GatherUnmarkedEntities`). The engine had taken the centres within the radius.

**What changes is retail's AI.** Before each build, retail's `ProcessBuildCommand` (`platoon.lua`) calls `EngineerTryRepair` (`AI/aiutilities.lua`), which looks for a structure of the kind it is about to build within a radius of 1 of the site and repairs it *instead of* building:
- a structure beside the site is now on the same cell, so the engineer is ordered to repair its finished, healthy neighbour, the site stays empty, and the build comes round again;
- a scratch hook counted it on SCMP_009, seed 4242, over 6,000 ticks: 644 repairs instead of a build, against 248 builds. The commit before 0c2bead5 has 4 (each on its own site, a structure left unfinished) against 472 builds;
- the neighbour found is a T1 power generator 2 to 2.8 units away, finished and at full health.

This is retail's loop, and FAF met it under Moho: its AI Update V8 (FAF #3114, Uveso, 2020) says "Units now get capture/reclaim and buildcommands queued instead of looping the ProcessBuildCommand", and since then FAF's `ProcessBuildCommand` repairs and builds, not one or the other. FAF's AIs here are unaffected. The scripts aren't patched; the engine now gathers as Moho's does, so retail's AI builds as it would under Moho, as far as faf-re shows. An original-Moho game would settle it.

The bench's baselines (2026-10-07) were recorded before #591, so `bench.py check` would now report the games' changes; they need recording again.
